About Maka-Villa

Maka-Villa is a bar, restaurant and accommodation destination in Murang'a, Kenya.

Whether you are looking for a delicious meal, nyama choma, refreshing drinks, a place to relax, or somewhere comfortable to stay, Maka-Villa welcomes you.
